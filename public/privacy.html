Privacy policy

Brilliancy Research appointment booking · Last updated 25 October 2026

Who we are

Brilliancy Research, Level 13, Fareast Tower, 35 Topkhana Road, Dhaka 1000, Bangladesh. Contact: brilliancycomms@gmail.com.

What we collect when you book

  • Your name, phone number, email address, university or institution, and the note you write.
  • The service, meeting type and time you choose, and the name of the person who referred you, if any.

How we use it

  • To schedule your appointment and add it to our office Google Calendar, with you invited as a guest.
  • To send you a confirmation, a reminder and any cancellation notice by email.
  • To let the staff member who referred you know that you booked (name, service and time only).
  • For internal records and referral reports. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Google Calendar access

Our booking system connects only to Brilliancy Research's own office Google account. It reads free/busy times to show available slots and creates, updates or deletes the appointment events it booked. It does not read the content of other calendar events and does not access any client's Google account. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

Booking data is stored in our Supabase database and processed by our Cloudflare service. Emails are sent through Brevo. Only Brilliancy Research administrators can view bookings.

Your choices

You can cancel an appointment with the link in your confirmation email. To see, correct or delete your data, email brilliancycomms@gmail.com.
